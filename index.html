How it works

One simple weekly moment — and nothing to learn.

The whole thing is one weekly loop. You don’t manage an app; you answer a question that arrives, and talk.

1

The prompt arrives

A question chosen for your grandchild’s age and interests, in the private space you share.

2

You talk it through

A few words, a voice note, or a video — back and forth, at your own pace, whenever the week allows.

3

The story is kept

Stories from grandparent and grandchild alike are saved, along with the milestones along the way.

4

It comes back around

Your grandchild gets the next question to ask you, so the ritual keeps itself going — without anyone nagging.

Simple on purpose

Built so the least confident person in the family can use it.

Large text by default, words on every button rather than icons, and nothing to configure — one thing on the first screen, no feed to scroll, no wrong button to press. And a real person, right here in The Villages, answers the phone if anything ever sticks.

Why it’s different

A private family space — not another open platform.

One private circle, invited by the family — up to ten people, added by phone number. No feed, no discovery, no strangers, no advertising.

Simple

The grandparent can set it up alone. No one in the family becomes tech support.

Safe

Age-appropriate prompts, and a parent approves before a child joins.

Secure

Private by default. Encrypted, no public feed, no strangers, nothing sold — ever.

Built to follow COPPA — because these are children.

Nothing about a child is collected until a parent gives their consent, and parents can review, delete, or turn it off at any time. Children’s privacy is the floor, not the ceiling. Read our Privacy Policy · A note for parents.
