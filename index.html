What Grandbabiez is

One simple weekly moment — and nothing to learn.

Each week, a question arrives — chosen for your grandchild’s age, and something you actually want to know. They answer in their own time, with a call, a voice note, or a video. You never have to think of what to say first, and nobody has to organize it.
