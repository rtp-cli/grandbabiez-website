Privacy

Your family’s privacy.

Grandbabiez is a private place for one family to share a weekly moment. Your family’s stories belong to your family — not to advertisers, not to strangers, not to us. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and the control you always have.

Effective date: October 16, 2026  ·  Who we are: [Grandbabiez legal entity name], [company mailing address].

The short version

If you read nothing else, here’s the whole spirit of it:

  • Your circle is private by default. Nothing is public, and there are no strangers.
  • We collect only what we need to make the weekly moment work.
  • We never sell your information, and we don’t run ads.
  • A parent is in control of everything about a child, and can pause or delete anytime.
  • You can ask us to delete your information whenever you like.

What we collect, and why

We keep this short on purpose. To run your family’s weekly moment, we collect:

  • The basics to set up your circle — a first name and phone number for each member you invite, so we can reach the right people. An email address only if you choose to give one.
  • The weekly answers — the calls, voice notes, and videos your family shares, which become your keepsake.
  • A little about each grandchild — their first name, age, and interests, so the questions we send actually fit them.
  • Basic account information — enough to keep your circle working and secure.

We do not collect home addresses, precise location, or anything we don’t need for the service.

How we use it

We use your family’s information for one thing: to deliver the weekly moment and keep the keepsake safe for your circle. That means sending the right prompt to the right person, delivering answers within your circle, keeping your family’s record, and reaching you about your account or with your consent. We do not use your information to advertise to you, and we do not build profiles to sell.

Who can see it

Your information stays inside your circle. The people you invite can see what’s shared there, and no one else. There is no public feed and no way for a stranger to find your family.

A small number of trusted service providers help us run Grandbabiez — for example, secure hosting and processing. They act only on our instructions, only to provide the service, and are bound to protect your information. They are not allowed to use it for their own purposes. We’ll name our current providers (or their categories) here: [service providers to list].

We never sell your information, and we never share it for advertising. Ever.

Data Sharing

  • Customer data is not shared with 3rd parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
  • Mobile opt-in and consent are never shared with anyone for any purpose. Any information sharing that may be mentioned elsewhere in this policy excludes mobile opt-in data.

Children’s privacy — and a parent’s control

Grandbabiez is meant to include grandchildren, some of whom are under 13, so we follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and build a parent’s control into the heart of it.

  • A parent decides. A child is never added to a circle until that child’s parent has approved it and given their verifiable consent. Nothing about a child is collected before then.
  • What we collect from a child. Only the answers they share (voice, video, or a note), their first name, age, and interests. Because a video or voice recording contains a child’s image or voice, we treat it with the care the law requires.
  • A parent stays in control. At any time, a parent can review everything we’ve collected from their child, delete it, pause participation, and withdraw consent to stop any further collection or use. We’ll never make this hard.
  • We ask for no more than we need. If we can offer something without collecting a child’s information, we will.

The law allows some services to collect a child’s recording without a parent’s consent if it’s used once and immediately deleted. Because your family’s answers are meant to be kept as a keepsake, we don’t rely on that shortcut — we get a parent’s full consent first. To exercise any of these rights, a parent can reach us at the phone number or email below.

How we keep it safe

We protect your family’s information with safeguards suited to how personal it is — including encryption while it travels and while it’s stored, strict limits on who can access it, and regular review. We only work with service providers who can protect it too, and we get their written promise that they will.

How long we keep it

We keep your family’s information only as long as we need it to provide the service and hold your keepsake — never indefinitely. When you ask us to delete something, or when there’s no longer a good reason to keep it, we remove it securely. Our specific keeping-and-deleting timeframes are published here: [retention periods to confirm].

Your choices

You’re always in control. You can review the information we hold about your family, ask us to correct or delete it, pause or close your circle, and (for a parent) manage everything about a child. Just reach out using the details below and we’ll take care of it — a real person will help.

Cookies and tracking

We use only what’s needed to keep the service working and secure. We do not use advertising trackers, and we don’t let anyone follow your family around the internet.

Changes to this policy

If we ever make a meaningful change to what we collect or how we use it, we’ll update this page and — where a child’s information is involved — tell parents directly and ask for their consent again before the change takes effect.

How to reach us

If you have any question about your family’s privacy, or you’d like to review, correct, or delete information, please reach out. A real person answers.

Grandbabiez is a structured family app that helps grandparents and grandchildren who live apart stay close through weekly prompts, shared stories, photos, videos, and voice recordings. It is operated by Grandbabiez, Inc., a Florida corporation based in The Villages, Florida ("Grandbabiez," "we," "our," or "us"). Grandbabiez, Inc. is the business responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to personal information we collect through:

  • the Grandbabiez mobile apps for iPhone/iPad (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play);

  • our website at grandbabiez.com and any pages that link to this policy;

  • our phone line, email, and other support channels; and

  • events, sign-up forms, surveys, and outreach in The Villages and elsewhere.

Together these are the "Services."

What this policy does not cover

  • Apple, Google, and other companies whose services you use alongside ours. They have their own privacy policies (see Section 14).

  • Information you share outside Grandbabiez, such as a photo a family member saves to their own phone and sends elsewhere.
