Privacy Policy

Your family’s moments belong to your family.

Not to advertisers, not to strangers, not to us. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Grandbabiez is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS].

The short version

  • ✓We do not sell or rent your information. Ever.
  • ✓We do not run ads, build advertising profiles, or use tracking pixels.
  • ✓Nothing about a child is collected until a parent gives verifiable consent.
  • ✓Everything stays inside your family’s private circle.
  • ✓You can see, correct, or delete what we hold — by tapping once, or by calling us.

Our business is a simple subscription. That is deliberate: when the subscription pays for the service, your family’s moments do not have to.

What we collect

From the grandparent who signs up

Your name, phone number, and email address, so we can set up your account and reach a human being to you if something goes wrong. If you subscribe, our payment processor handles your card details — we never see or store the full number.

From the family circle

The first names and relationships of the people a family invites, and the phone numbers used to invite them. A circle holds up to ten people, and only the family can add someone to it.

About a child — only after a parent says yes

A child’s first name and age band, and whatever the family chooses to share inside the circle: answers to prompts, photos, drawings, voice notes, and video. We ask for an age band rather than a birth date because the age band is all we need to choose age-appropriate prompts.

Technical information

Basic diagnostic and device information so we can keep the app working and fix crashes. We do not use this to build a profile of you or to follow you around the internet.

What we never do

  • —Sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.
  • —Show advertising, or let advertisers reach your family through us.
  • —Build behavioural or advertising profiles of adults or children.
  • —Use third-party tracking pixels or advertising trackers.
  • —Make any part of your circle public, searchable, or discoverable.
  • —Use your family’s photos, voices, or stories in our marketing.

If we ever needed to change one of these, we would tell you first and ask, rather than quietly updating a policy page.

How we use what we collect

To run the service and nothing else: delivering the weekly prompt, carrying what your family shares between the people in the circle, keeping keepsakes safe, handling your subscription, and answering you when you call.

We also look at aggregate, de-identified patterns — how many families finish a prompt, say — to work out what is actually helping. That work never involves reading one family’s moments to decide what to build.

Who can see your family’s moments

The circle

The people the family invited. That is the whole audience. There is no feed, no discovery, no recommendations, and no way for someone outside the circle to find your grandchildren.

Us, rarely and reluctantly

Access inside our team is least-privilege: only the people who need it, only when they need it, with no casual browsing. In practice that means when you ask us for help, or when we have to investigate a safety problem or a technical fault.

Service providers

We use a small number of companies to host and deliver the service. They may only act on our instructions, they are bound in writing to protect your information, and they may not use it for their own purposes.

The law

If we are legally compelled to disclose something, we will — and we will tell the affected family unless we are legally forbidden from doing so.

Children’s privacy

The U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) sets the rules for collecting information from children under 13. We are building to follow it, and we treat it as the floor rather than the ceiling.

  • ✓Nothing about a child is collected until a parent gives verifiable consent.
  • ✓Parents can review, delete, pause, and withdraw consent at any time — with one tap or one call.
  • ✓Withdrawing consent stops collection and deletes what we hold about that child.
  • ✓We ask for the least we can: a first name and an age band.
  • ✓Prompts are written and reviewed for the child’s age band.
  • ✓We are evaluating an independent COPPA Safe Harbor certification for outside oversight.

Before anything is collected, a parent receives a direct notice explaining exactly what will happen. You can read that note in full on the parent notice page, and there is a fuller walkthrough for parents on the for parents page.

How we protect it

Your family’s calls, notes, and keepsakes are encrypted while they travel and while they are stored. Access is least-privilege and logged. We work only with service providers who can meet the same bar, and we get their written commitment that they will.

Security is not a one-time checkbox, so we review and improve as threats and best practices change. No service can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise — but if something ever went wrong, we would tell you promptly and plainly.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. A parent can do the same for their child, and can pause or withdraw consent at any moment. You can do all of this in the app with one tap, or by calling (408) 219-7677 and asking a person to do it for you.

We will not make you hunt through a settings maze, and we will not ask you to explain why.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your family’s moments for as long as your circle is active, because that is the point — the keepsakes are yours to come back to. If you close your account or withdraw consent, we delete what we hold within [RETENTION PERIOD], except where we are legally required to keep a record.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you directly rather than relying on you noticing a new date at the top of the page. For anything affecting a child’s information, we will ask a parent again rather than assuming the old answer still holds.

Talk to a person

If any of this is unclear, call us.

You will reach a person, not a menu. If something in this policy does not make sense, that is our failure to explain it, and we would like the chance to fix it.
