1. Who we are, scope, and defined terms

Who we are

Grandbabiez is a structured family app that helps grandparents and grandchildren who live apart stay close through weekly prompts, shared stories, photos, videos, and voice recordings. It is operated by Grandbabiez, Inc., a Florida corporation based in The Villages, Florida (“Grandbabiez,” “we,” “our,” or “us”). Grandbabiez, Inc. is the business responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to personal information we collect through:

  • the Grandbabiez mobile apps for iPhone/iPad (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play);
  • our website at grandbabiez.com and any pages that link to this policy;
  • our phone line, email, and other support channels; and
  • events, sign-up forms, surveys, and outreach in The Villages and elsewhere.

Together these are the “Services.”

What this policy does not cover

  • Apple, Google, and other companies whose services you use alongside ours. They have their own privacy policies (see Section 14).
  • Information you share outside Grandbabiez, such as a photo a family member saves to their own phone and sends elsewhere.

Defined terms

Grandparent

A person who sets up or joins a family circle to connect with grandchildren

Family Member

Any adult invited into a family circle, such as an adult child, aunt, uncle, or caregiver

Circle Organizer

The adult who created a family circle and manages its membership

Parent

A grandchild’s parent or legal guardian, who approves and manages the child’s participation

Child

A grandchild participating in a family circle with a Parent’s consent; under COPPA, a “child” is under 13

Visitor

Anyone who browses our website or contacts us without having an account

Family circle

A private group of up to 10 members on the current plan who share content with each other

Family Content

Anything a member creates or shares in a family circle: answers, stories, photos, videos, voice recordings, reactions, and messages

Prompt

A question or activity we send to family members, such as “Tell me about your first job”

Personal information

Information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to a specific person or household

Service provider

A company that processes personal information on our behalf, under contract, to help us run the Services

A person can hold more than one role. For example, an adult daughter may be a Family Member in her parents’ circle and the Parent who approves her own child’s participation.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal information in three ways: directly from you, automatically when you use the Services, and from other people or companies. We collect only what we need to run a private family app.

2.1 Grandparents, Family Members, and Circle Organizers

Account information. Your name, email address, mobile phone number, password (stored in hashed form), and the display name and relationship you choose, such as “Grandpa Joe.”

Profile information you choose to add. A profile photo, birthday, hometown, the interests and family values you want prompts to reflect, preferred language, and accessibility settings such as larger text or read-aloud.

Family Content. Everything you create or share in a family circle, including written answers, stories, photos, videos, voice recordings, reactions, and comments. Photos and videos may contain embedded information such as the date taken; we remove precise location data from uploaded media before it is shared.

Family circle information. Who is in your circle, how members are related, who invited whom, and circle settings.

Contacts. When you add a family member, you can type their phone number or choose them from your phone’s contacts. If you choose them, your phone shows you the list and sends us only the one person you pick. We never read, upload or store your contact list, and we never see anyone you did not choose.

Payments. Grandbabiez is currently provided free of charge. The only payment we take is the 50 cent charge used to verify a parent’s identity, described in Section 6. If we introduce paid plans, we will update this policy and tell you before the change takes effect.

Support and feedback. What you tell us when you call, email, fill out a form, answer a survey, or share an idea for the app. With your permission, we may credit you by first name and town when we publicly thank you for an idea.

2.2 Parents

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and relationship to the Child.
  • The information needed to verify that you are the Child’s parent or guardian and the record of your consent, including the date, method, and what you agreed to.
  • Your choices about the Child’s participation, such as which circle the Child joins and which features are turned on.

2.3 Children

Children under 13. We collect information from or about a Child under 13 only after a Parent gives verifiable consent (see Section 6). With that consent we collect:

  • the Child’s first name or nickname;
  • the Child’s date of birth, which we use to decide whether the Child is under 13 and therefore needs a parent’s consent, to choose prompts that suit their age, and to record their birthday as a family milestone;
  • an optional profile photo or avatar; and
  • the Family Content the Child shares, which may include the Child’s image, voice, or likeness in photos, videos, and recordings.

We do not ask Children under 13 for a last name, home address, school name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

Grandchildren aged 13 to 17. A grandchild aged 13 to 17 joins only when a family member invites them, and holds their own account. Because they sign in themselves, we also collect their mobile phone number, which we use only to send their sign-in code and to confirm they are the person who was invited. Otherwise we collect the same kinds of information as for a Child under 13, and we never use a grandchild’s phone number for marketing.

2.4 Website Visitors and prospective customers

  • Information you enter into forms, such as name, email, phone, ZIP code, number of grandchildren, how you heard about us, and messages to us.
  • Information you give us at events, club meetings, or by phone.
  • Votes or suggestions submitted through our feature roadmap forms.

2.5 Information collected automatically

In the app: device model, operating system, app version, language and time-zone settings, a device identifier used to deliver push notifications, crash and performance logs, and how features are used (for example, which prompts are opened, answered, or skipped, and when).

On the website: browser type, pages viewed, referring page, time spent, and approximate location (city or region) derived from your IP address. See Section 7.

What we do not collect automatically: precise GPS location, your contact list beyond the single contact you choose from your phone’s own picker, your photo library beyond the items you choose to upload, your microphone or camera except while you are actively recording, or your activity on other apps and websites.

2.6 Information from others

  • Invitations: when a member invites you, they give us your name and email address or phone number so we can send the invitation.
  • Family Content about you: other members may mention you in stories or include you in photos and recordings.
  • Payment processor: confirmation that a Parent’s verification charge went through.
  • Service providers: for example, confirmation that a sign-in code was delivered.

2.7 Sensitive information

Some Family Content may reveal sensitive details, such as a family member’s health, religious traditions, or a child’s special needs, because families choose to talk about these things. We treat all Family Content as private, use it only to provide the Services to your family, and never use it for advertising or profiling. We do not use voice recordings or photos to create biometric identifiers such as face or voice prints.

3. How we use information

We use personal information only for the purposes below. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3.1 To provide the Services

  • Create and manage accounts and family circles, including sending invitations.
  • Deliver weekly prompts and show each member’s Family Content to the rest of their circle.
  • Store Family Content so your family can return to it.
  • Provide accessibility features such as larger text, read-aloud, and captions.

3.2 To personalize prompts

We use relationships, ages, interests, values, and which prompts your family has answered to choose prompts that fit each grandparent and grandchild. Personalization stays inside the Services; we do not build profiles to share with anyone else.

3.3 To verify Parents

Process the 50 cent charge a Parent pays to show they are an adult (see Section 6.2), and answer questions about it.

3.4 To support you

Answer calls and emails, troubleshoot problems, help families get set up, and follow up on requests. If you call us, we may keep notes of the conversation so the next person who helps you knows the history.

3.5 To keep the Services safe and secure

  • Verify Parents and record consent.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, spam, unauthorized access, and misuse.
  • Investigate reports of inappropriate content and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and property of our users, Children, Grandbabiez, and others.

3.6 To improve and develop the Services

We study how the Services are used, such as which prompts families answer most and where people get stuck, to fix problems and design better features. Wherever practical we use aggregated or de-identified data for this. When we de-identify information, we take reasonable steps so it cannot be linked back to a person, and we do not try to re-identify it.

3.7 To communicate with you

Send service messages, such as new prompts, reminders, security alerts, and policy changes. For adults, we may also send news about Grandbabiez, events in your area, and requests for feedback; you can opt out of these at any time (see Section 8). We never send marketing messages to Children.

3.8 To comply with the law

Keep tax and business records, respond to lawful requests from government authorities, and meet our obligations under COPPA and other laws.

3.9 With your consent

For any other purpose, we will tell you what we plan to do and ask for your permission first, for example before featuring your family’s story in a testimonial.

4. AI-assisted features

Some Grandbabiez features use artificial intelligence (AI) to make it easier for families to share stories. This section explains what those features do with your information.

4.1 What the AI features do

  • Follow-up questions: suggest a next question based on an answer, such as “What was your teacher’s name?”
  • Story capture: turn a spoken story into written text, and keep the original recording so your family can hear the story in your own voice.
  • Question Back: help a grandchild send an age-appropriate question back to a grandparent.
  • Tone tips: offer gentle suggestions for talking about harder topics, such as memory loss, divorce, or a parent’s deployment.
  • Transcription and captions: create captions for videos and voice recordings.

4.2 How Family Content is processed

  • AI features process only the Family Content needed for the feature you are using, at the time you use it.
  • Processing is done by us or by AI service providers acting on our behalf under written contracts.
  • Those contracts prohibit providers from using your Family Content to train or improve their own AI models and require them to delete it once processing is complete, subject to limited security retention.

Anthropic generates conversation prompts. AssemblyAI converts voice recordings into text; only a grandparent can record a story, so a child’s voice is never sent to it. Where a prompt is about a grandchild, we replace the child’s name with a stand-in before the request leaves our systems.

4.3 Training

  • We do not use Children’s personal information or Children’s Family Content to train AI models.
  • We do not use any Family Content to train AI models without the express permission of the adults involved.
  • We may improve our prompt library using aggregated, de-identified information, such as how often a prompt is answered.

4.4 People stay in control

AI suggestions are only suggestions. Nothing AI-generated is shared with your family circle until a family member reviews it and chooses to share it. AI features do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects, such as decisions about eligibility, pricing, or access to the Services.

6.6 Keeping and deleting a Child’s information

We keep a Child’s information while the family account they belong to is open.

A child under 13 takes part through a profile their parent manages, so the parent deletes it on their behalf, at any time, from the app. If that parent deletes their own account and no other adult manages the child’s profile, the child’s profile and information are deleted at the same time. The app tells the parent this before they confirm.

A grandchild aged 13 to 17 has their own sign-in, so they delete their own account and information themselves, and nobody else can do it for them.

If the person who created a family circle deletes their account, the circle closes and everything shared in it is deleted: the stories, the conversations, the prompts and the milestones. Other members keep their own accounts and can still sign in; what they lose is the circle and everything that was in it. A child’s profile that existed only in that circle is deleted with it. The app shows the owner what will be lost before they confirm.

In every case deletion is immediate within the app and cannot be undone. Removing every remaining copy, including from our backups, completes shortly afterwards.

6.7 Security of Children’s information

We maintain a written information security program designed to protect Children’s information, with a named person responsible for it, regular risk assessments, and controls described in Section 11.

6.8 If we learn we collected information without consent

If we discover that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

  • Minor changes, such as clearer wording, take effect when posted.
  • Significant changes, such as a new use of personal information, will be announced by email or in the app at least 30 days before they take effect.
  • Changes affecting Children’s information that require new consent under COPPA will not apply to a Child until a Parent gives that consent.

We keep prior versions of this policy and will share them on request.
