Ideas & thanks

Thank you for the ideas.

Grandbabiez is being built with families, not just for them — and some of the best ideas haven’t come from us at all. They’ve come from grandparents, parents, and grandchildren who took the time to tell us what would make this better. Below are suggestions we loved so much they’re now on our roadmap for a future release. Thank you, truly, to everyone who shared one.

Planned for future releases

Ideas from our community, coming to Grandbabiez.

We start simple on purpose — one weekly moment. But these thoughtful suggestions are the shape of what comes next.

Planned

The doorway they grow up against

That pencil mark on the doorframe, every birthday — captured and shared, so a faraway grandparent sees each new inch and keeps the whole growing record over the years.

Suggested by Fatima — Lahore, Pakistan
Planned

Front-page news from the day they were born

The newspaper’s front page from your grandchild’s birthday — a keepsake and a story starter, so you can show them what the world looked like the day they arrived.

Suggested by Roseanne — Pinellas County
Planned

For a child who doesn’t always speak

Ways to take part without talking — drawing, typing, choosing, or a wave — so a grandchild with selective mutism is just as much a part of the weekly moment.

Suggested by Mark — The Villages, FL
Planned

Built for children with special needs

Prompts, pacing, and ways to answer that adapt to each child — so a grandchild with special needs is fully part of the weekly moment, on their own terms.

Suggested by Cheryl — The Villages, FL
Planned

Gentle support for memory changes

Repeatable, reassuring prompts and tone guidance for families navigating a grandparent’s memory loss — so the connection stays warm even as things change.

Planned

Welcoming blended families

Prompts and circle settings that make step-grandchildren and new family shapes feel just as much a part of things from day one.

In honor of Barbara & Dean
Planned

A mode for distance and deployment

Extra care for military families and long separations — keeping a steady thread going when a parent or grandparent is far away for a while.

In honor of Rick, Scott, and all of the others — thank you!
Planned

The family pets, too

Prompts that bring in the animals a family loves — because a pet is often a child’s easiest, happiest thing to talk about, and a warm bridge across the miles.

In honor of Raider, Mika, and Luka
Planned

A printed keepsake book

Turn a year of answers and stories into a real book the family can hold, share, and keep on the shelf.

Planned

“Show me” photo & video prompts

Questions that invite a picture or a short video, not just words — a drawing, a school project, the view out the window today.

Planned

Games to play together

Simple, turn-based games a grandparent and grandchild can pass back and forth whenever the week allows — a guessing game, a would-you-rather, a photo scavenger hunt. Another warm, playful reason to reach out, especially for the kids who open up more through play than through questions.

Planned for a future release. We can’t promise exact timing — we’ll build these as carefully as we’ve built the first weekly moment.
