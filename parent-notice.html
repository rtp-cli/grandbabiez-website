A note for parents

Before [child’s first name] joins your family’s circle.

[Grandparent’s name] has set up a private Grandbabiez circle and would love [child’s first name] to be part of it. Because your child is under 13, nothing about them is collected until you say yes. Here’s exactly what that means — it takes a minute to read.

We’re sending you this note because [grandparent’s name] gave us your contact information so we could ask for your permission. That’s the only reason we have it.

What Grandbabiez is

Once a week, a friendly question arrives — chosen for your child’s age and interests — and they answer whenever it suits them, with a short call, a voice note, or a video. It stays inside your family’s private circle and becomes a keepsake you can all look back on. There’s no feed, no strangers, and no advertising.

What we’d collect from your child

Only what’s needed for that weekly moment:

  • their first name, age, and interests, so the questions fit;
  • the answers they choose to share — a voice note, video, or written reply (which may include their voice or image).

We would not collect their home address, their precise location, or anything beyond what the weekly moment needs.

How we’d use it

We use it for one purpose: to deliver the weekly question and keep your family’s keepsake safe inside your circle. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it outside your circle except with the trusted providers who help us run the service securely and on our instructions.

Your consent is required — and you stay in control

We will not collect anything from your child unless you give your consent first. And your say-so doesn’t end there. At any time, you can:

  • review everything we’ve collected from your child;
  • delete it;
  • pause your child’s participation; and
  • withdraw your consent, which stops any further collection or use.

You can read the full details anytime in our Privacy Policy.

How to give your consent

When you’re ready, here’s how to say yes — and we’ll confirm it’s really you, the parent, before your child joins:

  • 1Tap the button below (or call us) to confirm you’re [child’s first name]’s parent or legal guardian. [We’ll verify this using our chosen method — for example, a quick step with a trained member of our team by phone or video.]
  • 2Review what will be collected (it’s the short list above) and give your consent.
  • 3That’s it — your child can join the circle, and you can change your mind anytime.
