Direct notice to parents

The note we send parents.

Before a single thing is collected about a child, their parent gets this. We are publishing it here so you can read it before anyone asks you to decide.

Why this page exists

Most services bury the moment of consent inside a sign-up flow, where nobody reads it. We would rather you saw the exact wording in advance, with no account, no countdown, and nothing to click.

A parent receives this note directly — not a link to a policy, not a checkbox — and nothing about their child is collected until they respond to it.

The note, in full

Hello — [GRANDPARENT NAME] would like to start a Grandbabiez circle with [CHILD FIRST NAME].

Before anything begins, the law says we need your permission, and so do we. Here is exactly what would happen.

What we would collect about your child

Their first name and age band, and whatever your family chooses to share inside the circle — answers to the weekly prompt, and, if you allow it, photos, drawings, voice notes, or video. We do not ask for a birth date, an address, a school, or a phone number for your child.

Why

So that a grandparent who lives far away can share one small thing with your child each week. The age band lets us choose prompts written for their age. That is the entire purpose.

Who would see it

Only the people in your circle — up to ten, all invited by your family and approved by you. Nothing is public, nothing is searchable, nothing is sold, and there is no advertising. We do not use your child’s information for any purpose other than running this service.

What you can do, at any time

Review everything we hold about your child. Correct it. Delete it. Pause the circle. Withdraw your consent entirely — which stops collection and deletes what we hold. One tap in the app, or one phone call to (408) 219-7677.

If you say no

Nothing is collected and the circle does not start. No one will ask you twice, and we will not email you about it again.

To agree, [CONSENT MECHANISM]. To ask a question first, call (408) 219-7677 and a person will answer. — [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS]

What happens next

If a parent agrees

The circle opens, the parent chooses what their child can share, and the first weekly prompt arrives. The parent keeps every control listed in the note, permanently — consent is not a one-time gate that then disappears.

If a parent says nothing

Nothing happens. Silence is not consent. The circle does not start, no information about the child is collected, and we do not follow up repeatedly.

If a parent changes their mind

Withdrawing consent takes one tap or one call, stops collection immediately, and deletes what we hold about that child. No exit interview, no retention offer.

The rest of the detail

This note is deliberately short, because a notice nobody finishes reading protects nobody. The full account of what we collect, how we protect it, and who can see it is in our Privacy Policy, and there is a plain-language walkthrough on the for parents page.

Questions before you decide?

Call and ask. You will reach a person who can answer for your family specifically, and saying no is a perfectly good answer.
