Platform Overview

Grandbabiez is a private family app connecting grandparents and grandchildren. The Platform is accessible via mobile application and a companion website.

The Platform is organized around private family networks (each, a “Family Circle”). The adult who initially creates a Family Circle serves as the “Family Administrator.” The Family Administrator invites and approves family members, manages Family Circle settings, and may remove members at any time. Only individuals who have been invited and approved by a Family Administrator may join a Family Circle.

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account, or revoke access to any Family Circle, at our sole discretion, including for violation of these Terms, fraudulent activity, or any conduct we deem harmful to the Platform or its users.
